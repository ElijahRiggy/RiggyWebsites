06 / Questions

FAQ

I don't have a domain or hosting — can you help?

Yes. If you don't already have a domain, I'll walk you through picking one and getting the site connected to it. I also offer optional monthly hosting & care if you'd rather not deal with any of it yourself.

Do I have to pay monthly for hosting?

No. It's completely optional — you can host the site yourself or with any provider you like at no extra cost to me. The monthly plan is just there if you'd rather I handle hosting, uptime, and small updates for you.

How long does the free mockup take?

Usually a couple of days. I look at your business and what similar businesses do well before building anything.

Can I update the site myself later?

Depends on the build, but I can set it up so small text and photo changes don't require coming back to me every time. Or the monthly hosting & care plan includes small updates for you.

What if I want changes after I see the mockup?

Every package includes at least one round of revisions — this is meant to fit your business, not a template.
